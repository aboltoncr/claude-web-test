PAGE SUMMARY:
This page is a plot summary of "$100M Money Models" by Alex Hormozi (2025), the third book in his business trilogy following "$100M Offers" and "$100M Leads." The book's central argument is that a deliberate sequence of offers — called a "Money Model" — can make a business so profitable within 30 days of customer acquisition that cash flow never constrains growth. Hormozi draws on his own entrepreneurial journey (from sleeping on a gym floor to $100M net worth by age 31) to illustrate how layering specific offer types compounds revenue exponentially.

KEY POINTS:
- A "Money Model" is built from four offer types in sequence: Attraction Offers (turn strangers into customers via free/discounted entry points), Upsell Offers (capture majority of profit at point of sale), Downsell Offers (convert rejections by changing payment terms or features rather than price), and Continuity Offers (generate recurring revenue).
- Hormozi's highest-value tactical insight: billing customers every four weeks instead of monthly yields 13 billing cycles per year instead of 12, increasing revenue 8.3% — which for a business with 20% margins raises annual profit by 41%.
- Businesses that fail to recover customer acquisition costs within 30 days spiral into debt; those that do can reinvest immediately and compound growth — doubling customer value, customer count, and payment speed multiplies growth eightfold.
- The book warns against implementing a full Money Model at once; instead, build one stage at a time, price new offers low to gather feedback, then raise prices until incremental revenue plateaus.
- Hormozi's Gym Launch case study demonstrates the approach in practice: starting with a single Decoy Offer reaching $476K/month in three months, then layering upsells, downsells, and continuity to reach $4.4M/month within 20 months.
