PAGE SUMMARY:
This page is a brutally honest book summary and review of Alex Hormozi's "$100M Leads,"
written by Danny Rusteen (aka "dannybooboo"), a blogger, author, and entrepreneur. The
reviewer covers the book's core frameworks — lead magnets, content creation, paid ads,
affiliate systems, and employee management — while offering his own candid opinions and
personal experiences alongside the notes. He ultimately awards the book 5 stars despite
skepticism about certain sections, particularly the paid advertising strategies, calling
it well-timed and valuable for growing a business.

KEY POINTS:
- Lead magnets should be "a complete solution to a narrow problem," delivered in a
  7-step process: identify the problem, figure out how to solve it, choose a delivery
  format, test the name/headline, make it easy to consume, make it excellent, and
  include a clear Call To Action.
- The management/hiring insight that stood out most to the reviewer: trading hours
  doing for hours managing compounds dramatically — delegating lets owners multiply
  output and build equity by separating themselves from day-to-day operations.
- The reviewer is skeptical of paid ads and warns that Hormozi himself admits losing
  money more times than making money on ads; he recommends only pursuing paid ads
  after all organic channels (content, referrals, affiliates) are already working.
- Affiliates are highlighted as a golden nugget — especially the idea of charging
  affiliates a fee to join (e.g. $4k–$8k), and structuring a tiered payout system
  tied to performance (e.g. 25%/50%/100% of CAC).
- A key content creation reframe: switch from "How to" to "How I" and from "This is
  the best way" to "This is my favorite way" — and note that "people don't have
  shorter attention spans, they have higher standards."

INTENDED AUDIENCE:
Entrepreneurs and small-to-mid-size business owners who are actively trying to grow

written for $100M+ operators, but found it valuable even as a smaller business owner.
